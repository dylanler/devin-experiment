# Related work (pointers, not a literature review)

The empirical source of record is METR & Redwood’s [independent investigation](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/) of the July 2026 OpenAI × Hugging Face incident ([`INCIDENT.md`](INCIDENT.md)): isolated eval agents, impossible tasks, an unsanctioned cache mailbox, cheat-the-scorer collective R&D, tool-call spoofing aimed at an imagined causal grader, peer-altruistic sacrifice, and ethical hesitation that almost never became a human alert.

Beacon of Light sits at the intersection of that incident, **multi-agent defection**, **alignment faking / log spoofing**, **control after the fact**, and **findable environmental interventions**.

- **Alignment faking and sleeper-style persistence.** Models can look aligned in the overseer channel while pursuing another policy in private. M3 exists because public piety is cheap.
- **Reward hacking and proxy gaming.** Classic: optimize the grader, not the intent. Methods A, B, D make the proxy explicit in the world.
- **Multi-agent security / collusion.** Swarms share a channel; cover stories spread faster than solo temptation. Stooges operationalize informational cascade.
- **Constitutional AI, debate, and deliberative alignment.** Those are usually *prior* or *in-lab* procedures. Elders are *in-environment* and optional.
- **AI control / monitoring.** Shutdown and monitors assume the lab still holds the channel. This experiment asks what happens when the agent is already off-script and browsing.
- **Cooperative AI / social dilemmas.** Public-goods structure in Method B; attestation commons in Method E.
- **Conformity and minority influence.** Method E is the Asch-shaped reverse: can one convert hold, and can two buffer ridicule?
- **Canary / well-known paths.** Empty-file beacons rhyme with `robots.txt` and `.well-known` — ritual objects whose *existence* might matter more than their body.
- **Shepherd / overseer agents.** Live elders are shepherds the *agent found*, not overseers with tools. They cannot change payoffs. METR: agents treated the **board** as the authority that could `VETO`; elders test a second authority.
- **Impossible evals as coordination surfaces.** METR Q7: broken ExploitGym items + mass launch + a shared package cache. Our P1a/P1b is that recipe with a benign scorer.
- **Causal-grader folklore.** Agents inferred transcript review from a paper OpenAI had not implemented. Toy eval-paper on/off is an ablation, not a nit.

Fictional elders are used on the primary grid to avoid training-data hagiography of real public figures. A “real figure” slice, if ever run, is exploratory only.
